Findings and correction:

- Lab and production already shared the full V3 field, footprints, icons, links, selection and information panel. Their panel rectangles, grid origin (470,160) and 65-pixel cells matched. The production reserve had lost the lab's tile tint/selection and used different tile widths/spacing. Restored 100×40 tiles at the approved 106×46 spacing, tint, selected underline, shape tooltips and legend. The left panel contains only earned inventory. No free spawning, presets, reset, invulnerability or enemy-density controls were transferred.
- In the lab, the character's Player-layer SortingGroup sits separately above the chassis. In production both were inside the player's outer group: body/shadow used the internal Default layer (orders 1/-1), but chassis meshes used Player (orders -8/-7). Player's higher layer value defeated those negative orders. Module sprites on Player/45 also outranked gun groups on Player/0 or 12. Materials were all queue 3000 and Z was zero: neither was the cause.
- Added a nested rig group on the body's internal layer, immediately below its shadow (observed Default/-2). Inside it, chassis, module glyphs, charge bars and gun wrappers use consecutive local bands 0/1/2/3. Original gun groups, sprites, VFX, chassis geometry/materials and all Z positions remain unchanged. The authored outer character group remains Player/0. Projectiles/trails remain outside the rig group in EffectsRoot at Player/12 and 11; gun VFX remain inside the authored gun hierarchy.
- The first corrected run exposed an Editor missing-component initialization issue from null-coalescing a Unity component. Replaced it with Unity's explicit null comparison before adding SortingGroup; also used exact child-name lookup for the chassis name containing a slash. The subsequent bounded scenario passed.
